# Reading

Started 25 Nov 2026. Not a roadmap. Grouped by theme, not by order. Pick whatever pulls you.
★ = easy entry point.

Field, in short: natural philosophy, or "process cosmology". Crosses philosophy of time,
foundations and philosophy of physics, complexity science / cosmic evolution, and process
philosophy / philosophy of religion.

Current position: growing block universe, with reality as the limit of the process.
Path so far: pantheism → panentheism → process → growing block.

---

## 1. Time and becoming

- [x] ★ **Fay Dowker, "Past, Present and Future: The Science of Time"** (talk). Causal sets and whether becoming is real. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LAFymPdAg5I
- [ ] **Crystallizing block universe**: Ellis & Rothman, "Time and Spacetime: The Crystallizing Block Universe" (paper, 2010). Growing block with quantum outcomes as its edge.
- [ ] **George Ellis, *How Can Physics Underlie the Mind?*** (book, 2016). Top-down causation: how higher tiers act back on lower ones.
- [ ] **Lee Smolin, *Time Reborn*** (book, 2013), then **Unger & Smolin, *The Singular Universe and the Reality of Time*** (2014). Time is real; even the laws may evolve.

## 2. Physics foundations

- [ ] **Causal set theory**. Reference: Surya, "The causal set approach to quantum gravity", *Living Reviews in Relativity* (2019). Dip in, don't read straight through.
- [ ] ★ **Krioukov et al., "Network Cosmology"** (paper, *Scientific Reports*, 2012). Causal sets as growing networks.
- [ ] **Quantum gravity**: the umbrella over this whole bucket, not a separate item.
- [ ] **Everpresent Λ and the DESI results**: fluctuating dark energy, causal sets' one testable bet.
- [ ] **Freeman Dyson, "Time Without End"** (paper, 1979). Can the process continue forever?
- [ ] **David Wolpert, "Physical Limits of Inference"** (paper, 2008). Why no system can fully model the universe it is in.

## 3. Emergence and the tiers

- [ ] ★ **Ladyman & Wiesner, *What Is a Complex System?*** (book, 2020)
- [ ] **Ladyman & Ross, *Every Thing Must Go*** (book, 2007). Start with chapter 1.
- [ ] **Daniel Dennett, "Real Patterns"** (paper, 1991). The idea behind Ladyman's "rainforest realism".
- [ ] **Maynard Smith & Szathmáry, *The Major Transitions in Evolution*** (book, 1995). New information media at each transition.
- [ ] **Howard Pattee, "The Physics of Symbols: Bridging the Epistemic Cut"** (paper, 2001). The record/body split behind the recursion idea.
- [ ] **Terrence Deacon, *Incomplete Nature*** (book, 2011). How purpose and meaning emerge from physics.
- [ ] **Sara Walker, *Life as No One Knows It*** (book, 2024). Assembly theory, a candidate way to measure tiers.
- [ ] **Basal cognition**: Michael Levin and Pamela Lyon's papers. The life-mind bridge.
- [ ] **Evan Thompson, *Mind in Life*** (book, 2007). Life-mind continuity.
- [ ] **Gould, *Wonderful Life*, versus Conway Morris, *Life's Solution*.** Contingency versus convergence: does the individual path matter?
- [ ] **Chaisson, *Cosmic Evolution*; Azarian, *The Romance of Reality*; Dempsey's writing.** Source of the tier table (matter, life, mind, culture, Ω), which this project extends to six tiers with Α.

## 4. Process cosmology and meaning

- [ ] **Samuel Alexander, *Space, Time and Deity*** (1920). Deity as the next emergent level, the original Ω.
- [ ] **Whitehead, *Process and Reality*** (1929). Hard going. Approach through a guide or through Hartshorne.
- [ ] **Charles Hartshorne**: process panentheism made readable.
- [ ] **Peirce, "The Architecture of Theories"** (essay, 1891). Evolutionary cosmology; reality as the limit of inquiry.
- [ ] **William James, *A Pluralistic Universe*** (1909). For the "singular or multiple" question.
- [ ] **Teilhard de Chardin, *The Phenomenon of Man*** (1955). The Omega Point.

## 5. Counterweights (views that disagree)

- [ ] **Carlo Rovelli, *The Order of Time*** (book, 2018). Readable case that time and becoming are less fundamental than they feel.
- [ ] **Ladyman's attack on armchair metaphysics** (*Every Thing Must Go*, ch. 1). The test the Ω ideas need to pass.

## Ideas worth keeping

- **Pairs pattern**: Matter (live) → Life (record: DNA); Mind (live) → Culture (record: symbols). Each record outlives and rebuilds what it describes.
- **Closure test**: a record tier is real when it encodes the machinery that reads it (von Neumann).
- **Ω and the Omega Point**: Ω is everything after culture, open-ended, and may hold several tiers. Teilhard's Omega Point is its far end, if there is one. Reality-as-limit is the Omega Point.
- **Growing block + limit**: if growth stops when free energy runs out, the limit is the finished block. Panentheist while growing, pantheist at the end.
- **Bridge to Ω**: interpretability, alignment, verification, governance. The "chemistry" between culture and whatever comes next.
