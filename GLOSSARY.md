# Becoming

The words this project uses for the universe and for how it is written about. They carry the cosmology, so they change only with the owner's approval.

## The ladder

**Becoming**:
The whole process by which reality grows, from its first events up through every tier.
_Avoid_: progress, evolution (for the whole process)

**Ladder**:
The six tiers in order, from Α at the bottom to Ω at the top.

**Tier**:
One level of the ladder, with its own kind of thing. There are six: Α, matter, life, mind, culture and Ω.
_Avoid_: rung, span, level, layer, stage

**Branch**:
A part of a tier that grows sideways from it, made of that tier's own kind of thing, with no new kind of thing in it: stars in matter, ecosystems in life. Each branch belongs to one tier and has its own entry. A branch may leave its mark on another tier, and its entry says which.
_Avoid_: stage, sub-tier, offshoot

**Open-ended**:
Said of a tier with no known outer edge. Α and Ω are open-ended.

**Α (Alpha)**:
The tier before matter: spacetime becoming, before anything has mass.
_Avoid_: the ground, the substrate, the beginning

**Alpha Point**:
The first spacetime atom, if there is one. There is no "before" it, since time is the order between the atoms.
_Avoid_: the Big Bang (a different, narrower idea)

**Matter**:
The tier that begins when particles first have mass, and so can first keep time.

**Life**:
The tier whose record is genes.

**Mind**:
The tier of living things that model their world. Where life ends and mind begins is an open question.

**Culture**:
The tier whose record is symbols.

**Ω (Omega)**:
Everything after culture. It may hold several tiers, each named only once it is found.
_Avoid_: the next tier, the end

**Omega Point**:
Teilhard's endpoint: the far end of Ω, if there is one.
_Avoid_: Ω (that is the whole tier)

## Records

**Record**:
A lasting encoding that outlives what it describes and can rebuild it: genes for life, symbols for culture.

**Pairs pattern**:
A live tier followed by a record tier: matter then life, mind then culture.

**Closure test**:
A record tier is real when its record encodes the machinery that reads it.

## Writing

**Entry**:
The piece written for one tier or one branch. Every entry has the same seven parts, and grows: it is first published when there is something to stand behind, then revised.
_Avoid_: post, article, chapter

**Established**:
A cited claim that is broadly accepted. Plain text in an entry.

**Contested**:
A cited claim that is a live proposal or interpretation, with who disagrees. Marked in an entry.
_Avoid_: speculative, controversial

**Stance**:
The author's own reading of a tier, set apart at the end of its entry.
_Avoid_: opinion, take, view

**Source**:
Something the author opened that a claim cites.

**Reading**:
An item on the reading list, which an entry may later cite as a Source.

**Open question**:
A question the project keeps open on purpose. Entries say how they moved one.

**Milestone**:
A finished set of entries that stands on its own. The first is the ladder itself.

**Zoom-in**:
A later milestone that goes deeper into one tier.
