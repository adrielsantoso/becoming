# How entries grow

Read, rethink, write. No drills.

1. **Read.** Each tier has a fact sheet in `study/<tier>/facts.md`: the readings in a sensible order, small enough for one sitting each, and the facts in them, cited to where they appear and marked Established or Contested. Claude makes it, from Sources it actually opened.
2. **Rethink.** Let the reading change how you see the tier. The fact sheet's questions, one for each part of an entry, are there to think with, not to answer on cue. Learn background as you meet it: when a reading leans on something you do not know, name it, and Claude adds a short Detour to the fact sheet saying what it is, why the reading needs it, and one source.
3. **Write a first version.** Once there is something to stand behind, draft the entry in your own words, with the sources closed. Claude fact-checks the draft against the fact sheet and marks what is wrong or thin, without rewriting it. Then publish.
4. **Revise as you read on.** When a later reading changes your view, change the entry and set its `updated` date. Note in Questions moved what shifted, and update the Open questions page.
