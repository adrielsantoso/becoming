## Then: look back

Which entries found readers, and which were the most alive to write? The answer picks the first zoom-in.

## Candidate zoom-ins

- **Matter:** the elements, one by one, from the stars that made them to what we build from them.
- **Life:** the major transitions.
- **Mind:** basal cognition, and the bridge from life to mind.
- **Culture:** industries and civilization.
- **Ω:** the bridge: interpretability, alignment, verification, governance.

## The long aim

A shared story of the universe that stays true to the evidence and is still worth revering. It is reached milestone by milestone, never aimed at directly.
