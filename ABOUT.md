# About

Becoming tells the story of the universe as it is still being made, in six tiers: **Α**, where spacetime grows; **matter**, from the first mass; **life**, whose record is genes; **mind**; **culture**, whose record is symbols; and **Ω**, everything after culture. The words are defined in the [glossary](https://github.com/adrielsantoso/becoming/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md).

## How to read an entry

Every entry has seven parts: the image, Arising, What's new, Contested, Stance, Questions moved, and Sources.

- **Plain text is Established:** cited and broadly accepted.
- **Pink boxes are Contested:** cited, but a live proposal or interpretation, with who disagrees.
- **The gold box is my Stance:** my own reading, kept apart from what the Sources say.

## Who makes it

I write every entry. Fact sheets, fact-checks, diagrams and the site itself are made with help from Claude, an AI. Every claim cites a Source I opened.
